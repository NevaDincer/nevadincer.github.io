  <div><span class="tag">research</span></div>
  <p class="entry-body">I spent the summer as a Research Intern at SINTEF Digital in Norway, working remotely under Dr. Arda Göknil. I built and compared single-agent, two-agent and four-agent setups for automated code review across three levels of automatic prompt optimization, holding the task, model and round count fixed so that only the architecture and the optimization level varied. I replicated VulTrial (arXiv:2505.10961, ICSE 2026) on its own benchmark with different models substituted in, and got the same model-specific failure patterns back in two independent runs weeks apart. Partway through I found that several early accuracy differences came from bugs in my own evaluation code rather than from the models, which turned out to be the most useful thing that happened all summer.</p>
