How to play
  1. The numbers next to a row (or above a column) are the lengths of the filled blocks in it, in order.
  2. Between two blocks there is at least one empty square.
  3. Fill the squares you're sure about, cross out the ones you know are empty, and keep going until every number fits.
3 2

One way to fill a row with the clue 3 2.

Tap or drag to fill. Right click, or switch to Cross, to mark empty squares.
